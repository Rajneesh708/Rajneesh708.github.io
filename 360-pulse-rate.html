The Core Framework

The CFB Framework.
Where the real work happens.

At the heart of 360° PULSE RATE™ is the CFB Framework – the three psychological structures that most directly govern leadership behaviour under pressure. Every leader has all three. The question is which one is dominant, and how it manifests in their specific organisational context.

C
Complexes
Inherited psychological structures – patterns absorbed from early experience that operate below conscious awareness. They shape how a leader responds to authority, failure, vulnerability, and conflict – without the leader knowing why.
F
Fears
Operational fears – not general anxiety, but specific, identifiable fears that arise in leadership contexts. Fear of being exposed. Fear of losing control. Fear of being seen to be wrong. These fears make the decisions before the leader consciously decides.
B
Biases
Cognitive patterns that distort perception and judgment in predictable ways – built through years of experience, success, and the mental shortcuts that come with both. The more capable the leader, the more entrenched the blind spots.

MECULS Personality Types

Six archetypes.
Each one precise. Each one actionable.

These are not abstract psychological categories. They are named, behavioural descriptions – drawn from real leaders in real organisations – each with a framing statement that makes the insight immediately recognisable and immediately useful.

The Straightforward Realist
Rejects diplomacy as manipulative – struggles to adapt communication style in leadership forums
The Pragmatic Executor
Executes with discipline – micro-manages because trusting others feels like losing control
The Guarded Supporter
Loyal and dependable – collapses under CXO pressure because he does not trust his own abilities
The Protective Harmonizer
Creates harmony – but an unknown fear prevents her from taking hard stands when they are needed
The Conviction Holder
Holds strong convictions – resists feedback because being wrong feels like being worthless
The Guarded Controller
Does not trust anyone – because deep down, he does not trust himself
Each type is proprietary to MECULS – named through direct observation, not questionnaire. No two leaders receive the same type description. The precision is the point.
The Process

Four stages.
One complete transformation.

The 360° PULSE RATE™ is not a workshop series. It is a structured coaching architecture that moves from diagnosis to change – each stage building on the last, each one producing something the leader uses in real work before the next stage begins.

01
Personality Surfaced
Direct observation identifies the core psychological pattern – the complex, fear, or bias most significantly governing leadership behaviour. No forms. No questionnaires. The profile cannot be gamed.
02
Block Diagnosed
The MECULS Personality Type is named. The specific internal block is identified with precision – not as a category, but as a behavioural description the leader immediately recognises as true.
03
Responses Practised
Coaching sessions use the leader's actual organisational scenarios – not generic case studies. New ways of speaking, deciding, pausing, and taking ownership are rehearsed until they become natural.
04
Change Measured
A Final Assessment Report documents the shift – behavioural delta, coachability rating, Jungian typology mapping, and development recommendations. Observable change in real work is the standard.
