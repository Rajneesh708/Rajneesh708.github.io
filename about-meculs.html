The Engagement

What actually happens
inside a MECULS engagement.

Every engagement moves through four deliberate stages. Not a process imposed from the outside – the natural architecture of how lasting behavioural change actually occurs. First seen, then recognised in real time, then practised, then lived. Leaders who have been through it describe it as the first time coaching produced something that stayed.

See the Full Journey →
01
Awareness
The leader sees their personality profile in full – including the patterns they have never had language for. This removes the ambiguity that allows problematic patterns to continue unquestioned.
02
Recognition
The leader begins to notice the pattern operating in real time – in actual meetings, actual decisions, actual pressure moments. This is the stage most coaching programmes skip entirely.
03
Rehearsal
New responses are practised through simulations before being required in high-stakes situations. Behaviour changes when it has been rehearsed, not when it has been instructed.
04
Integration
The new response becomes the default. Not a conscious effort. Not a technique to remember under pressure. A new pattern that operates where the old one used to. This is the documented outcome.
