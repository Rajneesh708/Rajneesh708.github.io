Privacy Policy

Effective date: This policy is current as of the date of your visit. Last reviewed: 2024.

Your privacy matters to us. MECULS collects only the information necessary to provide our services and respond to your enquiries. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

1. Who We Are

MECULS is operated by Rajneesh Jain, located at D-13/172, Ground Floor, Sector-7, Rohini, Delhi – 110085, India. We provide leadership coaching, wellness counselling, meditation instruction, and related services through meculs.com.

For any privacy-related queries, contact: Jain.Rajneesh@meculs.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you provide it directly — for example, when you contact us, book a session, or register for the CV Portal.

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number (where provided)
  • Enquiry content — the content of messages you send us via email, WhatsApp, or website forms
  • Session-related information — notes relevant to delivering coaching, counselling, or meditation sessions
  • Payment information — processed via Razorpay, PayUBiz, or PayPal; we do not store card details on our servers
  • CV Portal data — information entered by registered users to build their CV (stored securely and accessible only to the registered user)
  • Website usage data — standard server logs and analytics (pages visited, time spent, browser type); no personally identifying information is captured in this way

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry or message
  • To deliver and manage coaching, counselling, or meditation sessions you have booked
  • To process payments securely
  • To maintain the CV Portal service for registered users
  • To send session reminders or follow-up information where you have requested this
  • To improve the quality of our services

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Confidentiality

Everything shared in a MECULS coaching, counselling, or meditation session is held in complete confidence. We do not share session content with any third party — including employers, family members, or other organisations — without your explicit written consent, except where required by applicable law.

This commitment to confidentiality applies from the first conversation.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — payment processors (Razorpay, PayUBiz, PayPal) and hosting providers who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements — if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process

6. Payments

MECULS accepts payments via Razorpay, PayUBiz, PayPal, Credit Card, and UPI. These payment services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not store your card or payment credentials on our servers. All payment transactions are encrypted and processed securely by the payment provider.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet our legal obligations. If you wish to request deletion of your data, contact us at Jain.Rajneesh@meculs.com and we will action your request promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for any use of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact: Jain.Rajneesh@meculs.com

9. Cookies

meculs.com uses minimal cookies necessary for the website to function correctly. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. Analytics data is used only in aggregate form to understand how visitors use the website.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS. Payment data is processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment providers.

11. Children's Privacy

Where MECULS provides services to children, all personal information is collected from and managed by the parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental consent.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of meculs.com after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
