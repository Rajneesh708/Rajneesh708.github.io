MECULS · Begin a Conversation

No pitch.
No deck.
Just honesty.

A direct exchange about what is actually happening – in your leadership context, or your life. Rajneesh responds personally to every message.

What this conversation is – and is not
Not a sales call. Not a qualification process.
Rajneesh listens first – to understand the actual situation.
No commitment required. No follow-up unless you ask.
Most people say this conversation brings unexpected clarity.
Complete confidentiality from the first exchange.
How to Reach Rajneesh

Two ways.
Both personal.

Email

Write when you are ready.

Send a short note – even a sentence is enough. Describe what is happening, or simply say you would like to talk. Rajneesh reads and responds personally to every message. No auto-replies. No team inbox. For corporates: a 30-minute exploratory conversation. For individuals: a 20-minute first conversation – no commitment required.

Write to Rajneesh
WhatsApp

A message is enough to begin.

If writing a long email feels like too much right now, a WhatsApp message works just as well. Write what is on your mind – in whatever way comes naturally. Rajneesh will respond personally. Available for first conversations, session bookings, follow-up questions, anything in between.

What Happens Next

Three steps.
Calm. Unhurried. Personal.

01
You write or message

A sentence. A paragraph. Whatever feels right. You do not need to have it figured out. You only need to begin.

02
Rajneesh responds personally

No auto-reply. No assistant. Rajneesh reads your message and responds himself – usually within 24 hours, often the same day.

03
A conversation – not a commitment

No pitch, no programme, no pressure. You are completely in control of what comes next. The first exchange is simply a genuine conversation.
