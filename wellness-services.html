One-to-one sessions for what you are actually carrying.

Seven wellness services, all per-session. You pay only for the session you book — no subscription, no programme to commit to, no fixed count to complete.

01Anxiety, Burnout & Emotional Weight

₹999 / session

Conversation that goes below the surface, paired with guided meditation that lets what was found settle. 60 minutes, one-to-one with Rajneesh.

Who this is for Adults carrying tiredness, going-through-the-motions, or distance from their own life that's hard to explain. Young people under pressure who can't quite name it.

After payment, you'll pick a session time on Rajneesh's calendar.

Read more

02The Pattern That Keeps Coming Back

₹999 / session

A Jungian-rooted conversation that goes one level deeper than “why is this happening.” Surfaces the belief — formed before you knew it was forming — that's quietly running every repeating pattern in your life.

Who this is for People at a crossroads who keep hitting the same wall in some area of life despite trying harder. No tests, no questionnaires — just conversation that goes deeper than usual.

After payment, you'll pick a session time on Rajneesh's calendar.

Read more

03Grief, Crisis & Relationship Loss

₹999 / session

One-to-one accompaniment for when something has come apart — grief, crisis, or a relationship breakdown. No fixed agenda. Rajneesh sits with what is true for as long as it takes to become workable.

Who this is for People in pieces — after a death, a diagnosis, a separation, or any rupture that has not yet found its shape.

After payment, you'll pick a session time on Rajneesh's calendar.

Read more

04Breaking Free of an Addiction

₹999 / session

Three things working together: conversation that finds the unfulfilled want underneath the addiction, meditation that lets you stay with the hurt without fleeing, and creative expression that gives the want a real place to land.

Who this is for People stuck in addiction — substance, behaviour, or relationship. Works with what the addiction was trying to fix, not just the addiction itself.

After payment, you'll pick a session time on Rajneesh's calendar.

Read more

05Concerned About My Child — Parent Conversation

₹999 / session

The first conversation — with the parent, not the child. What's been happening, how long, what's been tried. Rajneesh listens and tells you honestly whether and how MECULS can help.

Who this is for Parents whose child is restless, withdrawn, struggling at school, screen-addicted, or anxious — and who want a clear next step before anything else.

After payment, book your parent conversation. The child session is booked separately afterwards.

Read more

06Working With My Child — Child Session

₹999 / session

One-to-one sessions with the child. No agenda. No assessments. The child speaks if and when they feel safe — often, the parent notices, more honestly than they have in months.

Who this is for Children from primary school through secondary school. Booked only after the parent conversation. Sessions held online worldwide or in-person in Delhi.

After payment, pick a session time that suits both you and your child.

Read more

07Long-Held Pain That Won't Shift

₹149 / session

A 30-minute one-to-one guided meditation session (15 min listening and explanation, 15 min meditation). Designed to reach the deeper layer where old pain has been stored, for depression, chronic anxiety, anger, and physical symptoms that have not responded to other approaches.

Who this is for People carrying something for years that no other approach has shifted. Daily practice with periodic sessions to adjust as it unfolds.

After payment, pick a 30-minute session time on Rajneesh's calendar.

Read more
