Refer People — Get Connected in Return

When you refer people to Ikshu (the CV portal of MECULS) and they register with us, we connect you — free of charge — with an equal number of people from our network at a similar level to yours.

Refer 10 people ? we connect you with 10 people in return: 5 peers at your level, and 5 senior professionals who can open new doors for you.

Please note: This is a goodwill exchange, not a guarantee. We connect referrers with available members in our network — matches depend on who is registered at the time of your request, their consent to be connected, and timing. MECULS does not guarantee a specific number of connections, the seniority of those connections, or the timeline for making them. By participating, you accept that connections are best-effort and made in good faith. All connections are made via email ID only and are completely free of cost.
