GMR Group

Delhi, Hyderabad, Bangalore · Cultural Transformation

GMR Group engaged MECULS for a multi-year transformation programme spanning four entities – GMR Group, GMR Energy, GMRIT, and DIAL. The work operated at two levels: transforming organisational thinking culture for middle and senior management, and resolving CXO-level conflicts through a structured framework developed specifically for this engagement.

  • ETG → ITG model – Exclusive Thinking to Inclusive Thinking – for middle and senior management across all entities
  • Shifted leadership culture from siloed, competitive thinking to collaborative, enterprise-wide thinking
  • KLMW Framework – King, Love, Magician, Warrior – 12-scenario diagnostic for CXO conflict resolution
  • Individual coaching and recovery sessions with top-level management at GMR Energy and GMRIT Vizag
  • Delivered across three cities over four years

Strides Pharma – Most Recent Engagement
Corporate Engagement – September to December 2025

Strides Pharma

New DGM Cohort · Full 360° PULSE RATE™ Deployment

Strides Pharma engaged MECULS to coach its newly promoted DGM cohort – 15 Deputy General Managers across manufacturing, quality, commercial, finance, and engineering. This was the first full-scale deployment of the 360° PULSE RATE™ model from Baseline Profiling through to Final Assessment, with complete documentation delivered to HR.

  • Baseline Profiling Reports identifying MECULS Personality Type and core block for all 15 participants
  • Three structured phases – Baseline and Deep Profiling, Application and Reinforcement, Final Assessment
  • Individual and group coaching sessions delivered in parallel across the 12-week programme
  • Final Assessment Reports with behavioural delta, coachability rating, and Jungian typology mapping
  • Profile accuracy verified directly by participants at programme conclusion
  • 30 reports – Baseline and Final – delivered to HR leadership
