The recognition

Capability is real.
Recognition is uneven.

You have spent years building something real. The judgement to know when a meeting is going wrong before anyone else in the room sees it. The discipline to deliver under conditions most people would not stay in. The instinct to read a numbers deck and find the one cell that is hiding the answer. None of this came from a workshop. It came from work.

And yet recognition for what you have built does not arrive evenly. The people who post the most are not always the people who do the most. The people who network the loudest are not always the people who deliver the best. Capability is real; visibility is something else – and the gap between the two is where careers quietly stall.

This is the gap Ikshu was built to close. Not by asking you to perform on social media. Not by asking you to grind a job board. By making your real work, in plain language, visible to the people who can actually act on it.

The name

Why we call it Ikshu.

इ is a beej mantra – a seed sound, the first vibration from which a thing begins. इक्षु means sugarcane: real plant, drawn from earth, holding sweetness that nourishes rather than rots the body. The same word, in older Sanskrit usage, also carries the sense of seeing clearly – perception that sees through to what is actually there.

That is what this portal was named after. Not the processed, sweetened version of you that performs on a job board. The real plant. The capability you already carry, set down clearly, so the people who matter can see what is actually there.

This is what we mean when we say seen.

Three things, included.
No tiers. No upgrades.

When you create your account – free, two minutes – you get all three of these. Not a teaser version. Not a free trial that runs out. The full thing, kept free because the philosophy behind Ikshu does not allow for premium versions of you.

What goes into your profile

Eleven core sections.
Seven optional.

A serious profile reflects a serious career. The eleven core sections cover the substance of your professional life. The seven optional sections let you bring in the parts of yourself that most CV portals have no room for – mentorship, consulting availability, publications, volunteering, the work you have made that did not fit on a single line of a CV.

Who this is for

Working professionals –
in both directions.

Ikshu is built for working professionals who want their real capability to be visible to the right people. Whichever direction you are facing.

  • People looking for their next role – and people available for consulting work.
  • People looking for mentorship – and people willing to mentor someone earlier in the journey.
  • People early in their career – and people late in it, wanting to share what they have learned.
  • People returning after a break – pregnancy, illness, family responsibility, study.
  • People who do excellent work but do not post on social media.
  • People whose CV does not reflect the full shape of what they have built.
  • People who want a single link they can share, kept current, owned by them.
  • People who would rather build a serious profile once than perform their career every week.
In plain language

What this is not.

The category has lost a lot of trust over the years – and for good reasons. Five things, said plainly, so you know what you are signing up for and what you are not.

  • We are not a job board. We do not list vacancies, we do not run applicant tracking, we do not charge recruiters for your data.
  • We do not sell your email. Ever.
  • We do not have premium tiers. The free version is the only version.
  • We do not promise jobs. We promise visibility and a real connection or two.
  • We do not push notifications you did not opt into.
