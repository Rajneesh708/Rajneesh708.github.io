I How It Works

Three steps.
No intake forms, no chatbots.

Every booking goes through Rajneesh directly. There is no assistant, no scheduling bot, no automated sequence. The discipline of the practice begins at the first contact.

01
You send a request.

Use the form below. Name the service you are booking, your preferred dates and times, and a few honest sentences about what you are coming to the work for.

02
Rajneesh responds personally.

Within two working days, you receive a direct reply – not a confirmation email. If the work is a fit, a session is scheduled and payment details are shared. If it is not a fit, you are told honestly and, where possible, pointed to the right practitioner.

03
The session happens.

Online globally, or in person in Delhi. Payment is confirmed before the first session. From there, the work is direct, private, and serious.

IV Where the Work Happens

Online globally.
In person in Delhi.

Mode 01
Online – anywhere in the world.

Delivered over a secure video platform. Audio only is an option for sessions where the work benefits from reduced visual input. You receive the link after the session is confirmed. Nothing is recorded unless you explicitly request it.

Mode 02
In person – Delhi, by arrangement.

For people based in or travelling to Delhi. The address and logistics are shared after the first session is confirmed. In-person sessions are held in a private, appropriate setting – never a café or a public space.
