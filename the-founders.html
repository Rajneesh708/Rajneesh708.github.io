The Founders · MECULS

Two founders.
One standard of attention.

MECULS is led by Rajneesh Jain, who built its body of work, and Sneha Hasrajani, who keeps people at the centre of it. Delhi and Ahmedabad. Online across India and worldwide, or at your own premises.

Who leads MECULS

Meet the
founders.

Founder · Delhi

Rajneesh Jain

Twenty-two years of original research and practice in leadership, the human mind, and organisational analytics. Architect of the 360° PULSE RATE™ coaching model. Former Boston Consulting Group. Former India Head, Springboard Research.

Rajneesh’s profile

Co-Founder · Ahmedabad

Sneha Hasrajani

Keeps people at the centre of MECULS. Fifteen years of raising two children and building a home, and fifteen years of daily yoga practice. Founder of Ritatva, personalised yoga.

Sneha’s profile
