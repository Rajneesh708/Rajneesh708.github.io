Not a menu.
Thirteen applications of
one body of work.

Every service MECULS offers is the same discipline – pattern recognition, Jungian inquiry, and precise rehearsal – applied to a specific pressure in a specific life. Corporate or personal. Leader or child. The surface changes. The architecture does not.

I How to Read This Page

The services are not
independent offerings.

They are thirteen doors into the same house. A CEO working on decision clarity, a teacher working on authority, a parent working with a nine-year-old, and an adult recovering from a long illness are all using the same underlying method – the MECULS Personality Profiling System, the CFB Framework, and the 360° PULSE RATE™ architecture – shaped to their specific context.

Choose by context. The method beneath is consistent.

II Corporate Services

Seven services
for organisations.

Each delivered personally by Rajneesh Jain. No associates, no franchises, no generic programmes. What you read is what you receive.

01 · The Flagship

360° PULSE RATE™
Leadership Coaching.

The ICF-accredited methodology at the centre of the MECULS practice. Identifies the specific psychological block governing a leader’s behaviour and builds a precise pathway to change it. ROI of 450–750% documented at The Boston Consulting Group.

02

Personality Profiling –
MECULS System.

Built on Jungian typology. Maps cognitive functions, shadow patterns, and leadership blind spots. The diagnostic foundation on which every MECULS coaching engagement rests.

See the Diagnostic →
03

Executive Coaching –
Bespoke.

One-to-one coaching for senior leaders, fully customised to the individual’s organisational context, pressure points, and development goals. No template. No session plan reused.

See the Practice →
04 · Cohort Work

Corporate Programmes.

Group coaching, simulation programmes, and structured cohort engagements for leadership teams. Designed for CHROs managing newly promoted managers, senior leaders, and cross-functional teams.

Two flagship capabilities within this practice The Executive Edge – a 12-week structured programme with three phases of deep profiling, application, and documented outcome. The CFB Framework – Complexes, Fears, Biases – the diagnostic lens applied across cohort work.
See the Programmes →
05

Organisational Health
Consulting.

Diagnosing and resolving the behavioural and psychological patterns that create dysfunction at the organisational level – beyond individual coaching. Board-level work on culture, decision architecture, and leadership coherence.

06

HR Analytics &
Compensation Benchmarking.

Data-driven people intelligence. Compensation benchmarking across roles and markets, people analytics frameworks, and the translation of HR data into strategic decisions the board can use.

07 · Launched April 2025

Executive Search – built on psychological depth.

Finding the right person, not the most impressive person. Every search is built on the same profiling and CFB work applied to coaching – meaning the candidate presented to a board has already been mapped for the specific pattern that will serve the specific role. Not a CV match. A psychological fit.
