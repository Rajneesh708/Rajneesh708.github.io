I How to Read This Page

Not a menu.
A body of work,
read fifteen ways.

The services on this page are not independent offerings. They are doors into the same house – a chief executive working on the decision they keep getting wrong, a teacher working on authority, a parent sitting with a nine-year-old, an adult emerging from a long illness. The same diagnostic. The same discipline. The same person delivering it. The surface changes. The architecture does not.

Read the categories. Look for the pressure that matches what you are actually carrying. The right service will not be the most impressive one. It will be the most accurate one.

Choose by context. The method beneath is consistent.

III For the Person

Six services
for the person.

Delivered with the same depth as the corporate practice. Only now the subject is not a leader under quarterly pressure, but a person under the pressure of their own life – anxiety, addiction, a child, a long illness, the wish to be quieter inside.

10

Consciousness & Clarity Coaching.

The work of seeing clearly what is actually driving your decisions – and what you are avoiding seeing. Conducted in conversation. Held until the pattern becomes visible.

Signal

A recurring pattern you cannot name.

Mind Wellness – Mynd CARE.

A confidential professional space for adults carrying anxiety, burnout, unresolved emotion, or prolonged inner disquiet. Cure Anxiety, Restore Excellence – deliberate, structured, two-minds work.

Addiction Freedom.

Counselling.
