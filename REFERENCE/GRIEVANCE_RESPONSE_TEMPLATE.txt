GRIEVANCE ACKNOWLEDGEMENT EMAIL TEMPLATE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Save this somewhere accessible (Drafts folder in Gmail, Notion, or
just keep this file in your project folder). When a real grievance
email arrives, respond within 48 hours using this as a starting
point.

Customise:
  - {USER_NAME}     → their name (or "there" if unknown)
  - {DATE_RECEIVED} → date you got their email
  - {TICKET_REF}    → simple ref like GRV-2026-001 (incremental)
  - {SUMMARY}       → one-line restatement of their concern
  - {NEEDED_INFO}   → if you need anything more from them; else
                      delete that paragraph entirely
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Subject: We've received your grievance — Reference {TICKET_REF}

Dear {USER_NAME},

Thank you for writing to us on {DATE_RECEIVED}. I'm acknowledging
that I have received your message regarding {SUMMARY}.

This concern has been logged with the reference number
{TICKET_REF}. Please use this reference in any further correspondence
on this matter so I can locate it quickly.

I will personally investigate and respond with a resolution within
30 days, as committed on our Grievance Officer page. If the matter
turns out to be more complex and requires more time, I will write
back with a revised timeline before the 30 days are up — you will
not be left without an update.

[OPTIONAL — DELETE THIS PARAGRAPH IF NOT NEEDED]
To help me investigate accurately, could you please share
{NEEDED_INFO}? Once I have this, I can move faster.
[END OPTIONAL]

If at any point you feel my response is inadequate, you are entitled
to escalate the matter to the Data Protection Board of India, as
explained on our grievance page:

  https://meculs.com/grievance.html

Thank you for taking the time to bring this to our attention. We
take your privacy seriously and will work to resolve this fairly.

SIMPLE TRACKING SYSTEM
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You don't need a ticketing system yet. A Google Sheet with these
columns is enough until volume grows:

  Ref            | Date received | Email of complainant
  Brief summary  | Status        | Acknowledged on
  Resolved on    | Resolution notes

Status values to use:
  - Acknowledged    (you've replied with this template)
  - Investigating   (you're working on it)
  - Awaiting info   (you've asked the user for something more)
  - Resolved        (you've sent the final response and they accepted)
  - Escalated       (user took it to DPBI)
  - Closed          (no reply from user for 14+ days after final response)

Generate refs sequentially: GRV-2026-001, GRV-2026-002, etc.
Reset the counter each calendar year.

This sheet is your audit trail. If DPBI ever asks "what did you do
about complaint X", you can show timestamps and decisions.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT NOT TO DO
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Don't respond casually or apologetically without acknowledging
  the formal grievance. Treat every privacy email as a potential
  formal complaint, even if it sounds friendly.

- Don't promise more than you can deliver. The 48h / 30 days
  commitment is the official one. Don't say "I'll get back tomorrow"
  unless you actually will.

- Don't delete the email or the user's account in response to a
  grievance. If they ask you to delete data, do it through the
  proper deletion flow and KEEP a record (date, what was deleted,
  ref to grievance). DPDP requires you to be able to demonstrate
  compliance.

- Don't ignore a grievance. The 48-hour acknowledgement is the most
  important deadline — even if you have nothing to say yet, send
  the acknowledgement template.
