Find Your Core

For people who can't answer "what do I want."

A different kind of process for a different kind of problem.

06 · Career Investigation

₹699
4 weeks · Guided process · Includes 4 calls

I spent 40 years asking myself what I wanted from my career. I had every credential people told me to chase — top schools, ICF certification, Harvard certification, years at BCG and across industry. None of it answered the question.

At 51, I finally found my answer — not by asking myself, but by doing. By building things. By watching what pulled me in and what drained me. It turned out my core was “investigative mind” — I'm someone who creates new things through investigation. No questionnaire would have told me that. No coach would have either. I had to discover it through evidence.

This is the process I wish someone had given me at 35.

What it is
A 4-week structured discovery process for people who can't answer the question “what do I actually want from my career” — because most career advice assumes you already know. Most people don't. Instead of filling out personality tests, you do small experiments each week. We then look at what happened. The pattern emerges from the evidence, not from the questionnaire.
The 4 weeks
  • Week 1 A 60-min call where I don't give you answers. I ask the questions nobody asked you. You leave with 5–7 small experiments to do during the week.
  • Week 2 45-min debrief. What gave you energy? What didn't? New experiments based on what we found.
  • Week 3 Same. The pattern starts naming itself.
  • Week 4 60-min synthesis call. Using three weeks of evidence from your own life, we name what your core actually is.
Who it's for
People who feel stuck, who've tried tests and frameworks and “follow your passion” advice, and none of it worked. People who need someone who has been stuck — not someone who's read a book about it.
What you get
  • 4 video calls (60 + 45 + 45 + 60 minutes) over 4 weeks
  • Structured experiments designed for your situation
  • A written summary at the end naming what your core is
  • Full confidentiality — nothing leaves the conversation
Apply for Career Investigation · ₹699
Includes a short 20-minute screening call before we begin.
