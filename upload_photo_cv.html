📋 Photo Guidelines

⚠️ Required — your upload will fail or won't help us if you ignore these:

  • Format: JPG, JPEG, or PNG only
  • Minimum size: 400 × 400 pixels and 100 KB
  • Maximum size: 5 MB
  • Face clearly visible, looking at the camera
  • No group photos, filters, or sunglasses

✨ Recommended — for the best match quality:

  • Neutral or plain background
  • Good lighting on your face (no harsh shadows)
  • Recent photo (within the last 2 years)

💡 We’ll automatically optimise your photo for web display. Most phone photos work well — just upload, we’ll handle the rest.

Your CV Links by Domain*

Add up to 6 CV links — one per domain — so we can match you to opportunities across every area of your expertise. If you only work in one domain, add just one CV.

⛔

Read this carefully — broken links cannot be fixed later

  1. We accept ONLY 3 services: Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox. Links from any other source (iCloud, WeTransfer, personal websites, etc.) will be rejected.
  2. The link must point to a single CV document — not a folder containing your CV. Open your CV file first, then copy its URL from the browser address bar.
  3. Set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view" — not "restricted" or "private". This is the #1 reason links break.
  4. Test each link in a private / incognito browser tab before adding it here. If it doesn't open for an anonymous viewer, it won't open for our matching team either.
  5. Each CV must be a readable, text-based document — not a scanned image or photo of a CV.
💡
Tip: If you have multiple domains (e.g. HR + Recruitment, or Data Analytics + AI), add a separate CV for each. The Sub-domain field (optional) helps narrow your focus — for Data Analytics you might enter "Healthcare Analytics" or "Marketing Analytics" as the sub-domain.
