MECULS  ·  Zero State Meditation

Meditation
as medicine.

"A meditation discovered and taught exclusively by MECULS – not available anywhere else in the world."

The Core Truth

Where disease, addiction,
and depression actually live.

The Pain-Body

Suppression stored over years, living inside the physical body as tension, illness, compulsion, and despair.

Disease, addiction, and depression are the result of unfulfilled desires and suppressed feelings and emotions stored in the hidden consciousness. They do not live in the body alone. They live in what the body carries.

This is not a metaphor. The hidden consciousness – what most people experience only as vague unease, compulsive behaviour, or chronic physical symptoms – is a real, addressable layer of mind. It responds to specific practice.

Zero State Meditation neutralises suppressed desires, emotions, feelings, and fears in your hidden consciousness. It dissolves the pain-body that is present inside your physical body. The result is considerable relief in diseases, addictions, and depression.

With consistent practice, as guided by MECULS, complete cure is also possible.

Why ordinary meditation does not reach this

Most meditation traditions work with the conscious mind – relaxation, focus, breath. Zero State Meditation works at a different depth: the hidden layer where suppression has been stored. That is why it produces results that ordinary meditation cannot.

What Makes Zero State Different

This is not relaxation.
This is not mindfulness.

It is not relaxation, mindfulness, or breathwork

Relaxation reduces surface tension. Mindfulness observes the conscious mind. Breathwork regulates the nervous system. Zero State Meditation does something none of these can: it reaches the hidden consciousness – the layer below conscious awareness where suppressed material has been stored.

It addresses the hidden consciousness – not just the conscious mind

The hidden consciousness is where suppressed desires, unfulfilled expectations, unexpressed emotions, and unresolved fears reside. This layer drives behaviour, illness, and compulsion from below the surface. Zero State Meditation is the only practice known to reach and neutralise this layer directly.

It works where most meditation does not reach

Most meditation practices produce results in the conscious mind: more calm, better sleep, more focus. Zero State Meditation produces results in the body and the deeper mind: relief from illness, freedom from addiction, dissolution of long-held anger – because it addresses the source, not the symptom.

Discovered exclusively by MECULS – taught nowhere else

Zero State Meditation is not a variation of any existing tradition. It was discovered by Rajneesh Jain through direct exploration of the hidden consciousness over many years. No other teacher or institution teaches this method. It is entirely exclusive to MECULS.

What It Has Produced

Documented results
across six areas.

Depression

Considerable relief from depression symptoms through neutralisation of the suppressed emotional layer that sustains depressive states.

Addiction

Freedom from addiction – including smoking – by addressing the hidden need the addiction was fulfilling, not the habit itself.

Chronic Pain

Measurable reduction in chronic physical pain through dissolution of the pain-body stored in the physical body over years of suppression.

Anger

Permanent removal of recurring anger patterns – not anger management, but dissolution of the suppressed emotion producing the anger.

Relationship Conflict

Resolution of recurring relationship patterns by clearing the suppressed material that drives reactivity and projection in close relationships.

Disease Relief

Considerable relief in physical illnesses linked to suppressed emotional states – where the emotional root has been stored in the body.

Who This Is For

Five kinds of people
who have found relief here.

Adults experiencing depression, anxiety, or chronic stress – who have tried other approaches and found only temporary relief that did not last
People seeking freedom from addiction – including smoking, alcohol, and compulsive behaviour – who want to address the root, not the symptom
Anyone carrying unresolved anger or past pain – that has not shifted despite years of therapy, introspection, or spiritual practice
Those experiencing physical illness linked to emotional suppression – where conventional medicine has not found the underlying cause
People seeking deep inner peace and genuine connection to their authentic self – not surface calm, but settled, lasting stillness
A note before you begin

Zero State Meditation requires a sincere commitment to consistent practice – guided throughout by MECULS. A first conversation with Rajneesh is the right way to begin.

How It Works

Four stages.
Guided throughout.

01
The first conversation

Rajneesh listens to understand your specific situation – what you are carrying, how long, and what has and has not helped. This determines whether Zero State is the right beginning point for you now.

02
Initial instruction

The practice is taught directly by Rajneesh – it cannot be learned from a book or recording. The first session establishes the foundation and the specific practice for your situation.

03
Consistent guided practice

You practice daily, with regular sessions with Rajneesh to assess what is shifting, what is surfacing, and how the practice needs to evolve as the hidden layer clears.

04
Observable change

Over weeks and months, changes become observable – in physical symptoms, emotional patterns, compulsive behaviours, and the quality of inner stillness that begins to take root.

Begin Zero State Meditation

The hidden consciousness responds to the right practice. A single conversation with Rajneesh is enough to understand whether this is the right beginning for where you are now.
