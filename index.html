What MECULS Is

Not a coaching company.
A body of original work.

MECULS is a leadership development and people effectiveness practice. It is not built on adapted frameworks or licensed tools. Every methodology – the 360° PULSE RATE™, the CFB Framework, the MECULS Personality Profiling System – was built from scratch through 18 years of direct research, self-study in Jungian psychology, and client work at the highest levels of global consulting and Indian industry.

The work is delivered by one person – Rajneesh Jain – who brings the depth of a researcher, the precision of a BCG consultant, and the insight of someone who has spent two decades studying what actually governs human behaviour under pressure.

360° PULSE RATE™

The flagship ICF-accredited coaching model. Identifies the specific psychological block governing a leader's behaviour and builds a precise pathway to change it. Unlike MBTI or Hogan, this model goes beyond typing to deliver measurable, sustained behavioural change.
