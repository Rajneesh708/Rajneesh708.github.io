Two tracks.
One question underneath both.

MECULS works across two tracks – corporate leadership and individual wellness. What connects them is the same question asked at the start of every engagement: what is actually causing this, and at what level does it live?

The work that follows is built on that answer. Not on a framework applied in advance. On what is specifically, precisely true for this organisation or this person.

Every service below names who it is for. Read until something stops you.
